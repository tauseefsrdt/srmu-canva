H2
Questions Businesses Ask About Digital Marketing
H3
What does a digital marketing agency actually do?
A digital marketing agency helps businesses attract and convert customers through channels such as search engines, paid advertising, websites, social platforms and other digital channels. The right mix depends on the business, audience, goals and customer journey.
H3
Should my business invest in SEO or Google Ads?
SEO and Google Ads solve different problems. SEO builds organic visibility over time, while Google Ads can put your business in front of relevant searchers immediately. Many businesses can benefit from using both, depending on their goals and budget.
H3
Is SEO still important with AI search?
Yes. AI-powered search experiences still rely on information available across the web, and Google's current guidance continues to emphasise strong SEO fundamentals alongside useful, original content for its generative AI search experiences. (Google Developers)
H3
What is AEO and how is it different from SEO?
SEO focuses primarily on helping content perform in search engines. AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, focuses on making information clear, structured and useful for systems that provide direct answers to user questions. The two overlap significantly, and strong SEO fundamentals remain important.
H3
Why are my digital ads getting clicks but not enough enquiries?
Clicks are only one part of the customer journey. Poor conversion can come from targeting, the offer, ad messaging, landing page experience, lead form friction, follow-up or the quality of traffic. Effective lead generation looks at the entire journey rather than clicks alone.
H3
How do I know which digital marketing services my business actually needs?
There is no universal mix. The right approach depends on how customers find you, your sales cycle, geographic reach, customer value, competition and business goals. A business may need SEO, paid advertising, creative, landing pages or a combination.

H2
Questions Businesses Ask Before Investing in Digital Marketing
H3
Which digital marketing services does my business actually need?
There is no universal combination. The right mix depends on your audience, sales cycle, competition, geographic reach, customer value and growth objectives.
H3
Should I invest in SEO or paid advertising first?
SEO and paid advertising serve different purposes. Paid search can provide immediate visibility for relevant searches, while SEO focuses on building organic visibility over time. Businesses often use both, depending on their goals and resources.
H3
Is Google Ads better than Meta Ads for lead generation?
They reach people at different stages of the decision process. Google Ads can capture existing search demand, while Meta advertising can help businesses reach and influence audiences based on targeting and campaign objectives. The appropriate channel depends on the business and customer journey.
H3
Why am I getting website traffic but not enough enquiries?
Traffic does not automatically create conversions. The issue may involve targeting, messaging, offer clarity, landing-page experience, trust signals, forms or follow-up.
H3
Does SEO help with AI search visibility?
SEO fundamentals remain important because AI-powered search experiences use information from websites and the wider web. AI search optimisation should therefore complement—not replace—good technical SEO, useful content and a well-understood digital presence.
H3
What is the difference between SEO, AEO and AI Search?
SEO focuses on visibility in search engines. AEO focuses on structuring and presenting information so it can effectively answer user questions. AI Search refers to search experiences that use AI to interpret queries and generate or organise answers. There is significant overlap between the three.
H3
How much should a business spend on digital marketing?
There is no meaningful universal percentage or number. Budget should be considered alongside customer value, sales capacity, competition, target market, acquisition economics and the expected role of each channel.

H2
Questions Businesses Ask About Search Visibility
H3
Is SEO still worth investing in?
SEO can be valuable when customers use search to discover information, compare businesses or find services. Its value depends on the business, competition, search demand, customer economics and how effectively the website converts visitors.
H3
How long does SEO take to work?
There is no universal timeline. Results can depend on the site's existing authority and technical health, competition, content quality, search demand and the amount of work being implemented. SEO should generally be viewed as an ongoing growth activity rather than an instant advertising channel.
H3
How is AEO different from SEO?
SEO focuses broadly on improving visibility in search engines. AEO focuses more specifically on making information useful and understandable for answer-oriented search experiences. There is significant overlap, so AEO should generally complement strong SEO rather than replace it.
H3
How can I get my business mentioned in AI search results?
There is no guaranteed method for controlling whether an AI search system mentions a business. Businesses can, however, improve their digital presence by providing clear, useful and trustworthy information, maintaining strong SEO foundations and making their business, services and expertise easy to understand.
H3
Does SEO help with AI search?
Yes, SEO fundamentals remain relevant to Google's AI-powered search experiences. Google specifically advises site owners to continue following its established SEO best practices while creating useful, original content. (Google Developers)
H3
What is Local SEO and does my business need it?
Local SEO focuses on improving visibility for searches connected to a location. It can be particularly relevant when customers choose businesses based on where they operate or where the service is provided.
H3
Why isn't my website ranking on Google?
There can be many reasons, including technical accessibility, competition, search intent mismatch, content quality, site structure, authority and overall relevance. A proper SEO assessment should identify the specific issues rather than assuming there is one universal cause.
H3
How much does SEO cost?
There is no standard SEO price that applies to every business. Cost depends on factors such as website size, competition, geographic scope, technical requirements, content needs and the amount of ongoing optimisation required.
H3
Can a small business compete with larger companies in search?
It can, depending on the market and search opportunity. Smaller businesses can sometimes compete by focusing on specific services, locations, customer questions and areas where larger competitors have less relevant or useful content.

Questions Businesses Ask About Paid Advertising
H3
How much should a small business spend on Google Ads?
There is no universal budget. A sensible starting point depends on search demand, competition, customer value, geographic targeting, conversion rates and how many enquiries the business can realistically handle.
H3
Is Google Ads or Meta Ads better for lead generation?
They work differently. Google Ads can capture people actively searching for a solution, while Meta Ads can reach defined audiences based on campaign objectives and targeting. The appropriate platform depends on the business and how customers make decisions.
H3
Why am I getting clicks but no leads?
Possible causes include poor targeting, weak messaging, an unclear offer, an ineffective landing page, slow website experience, form friction or a mismatch between the advertisement and what visitors find after clicking.
H3
Why are my Meta Ads generating cheap but poor-quality leads?
Low cost per lead does not necessarily mean high-quality acquisition. Lead quality can be affected by targeting, campaign objectives, creative, qualification questions, offer positioning and follow-up.
H3
How long does Google Ads take to start generating leads?
Campaigns can begin receiving traffic shortly after becoming active, but meaningful performance evaluation requires sufficient data. Results depend on factors such as search volume, competition, targeting, budget, offer and conversion experience.
H3
What is a good cost per lead?
There is no single "good" cost per lead. A useful CPL depends on the value of a customer, lead-to-sale rate, sales capacity, margins and other acquisition costs.
For example, a ₹1,000 lead may be expensive for one business and inexpensive for another.
H3
Should I run Google Ads and Meta Ads together?
They can complement each other when each platform has a defined role. Google can capture existing demand, while Meta can help reach and influence relevant audiences. The decision should be based on the customer journey and economics rather than simply using more channels.
H3
Do I need a landing page for Google Ads?
Not always, but a dedicated landing page can be useful when the campaign has a specific audience, offer or conversion objective. The page can be designed around the exact intent behind the advertisement rather than sending every visitor to a generic homepage.
H3
How do I know whether my advertising is actually profitable?
You need to connect advertising data with business outcomes. Depending on the business, this may involve tracking qualified leads, sales, customer acquisition cost, revenue and customer value—not just clicks or impressions.
H3
How can I reduce my cost per lead?
Lowering CPL isn't necessarily the goal. First identify whether the existing leads are valuable. Improvements can come from better targeting, messaging, creative, offers, landing pages, conversion tracking and campaign optimisation.

What makes a good advertising creative?
