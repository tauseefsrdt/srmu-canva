5. QUICK ANSWERS
I would keep this extremely short.
H2
Before You Reach Out
H3
Do I need to know exactly which service I need?
No. Tell us what you're trying to achieve or where you're facing a problem. We can help identify which areas may be relevant.
H3
Do you only work with businesses that already advertise online?
No. Depending on the business, opportunities may exist across organic search, AI Search, paid advertising, creative, landing pages or a combination.
H3
Can you audit my current digital marketing?
Yes. A free audit can be used to identify potential opportunities across relevant areas of your digital presence.
H3
Do you manage social media accounts?
No. Redcanvass focuses on performance marketing, search visibility, AI Search/AEO, lead generation and campaign-focused creative rather than ongoing social media management.
That last FAQ is actually important because it pre-qualifies the wrong leads and reinforces your positioning.
